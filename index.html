Administrative Hierarchy & Key Duties

Deputy Commissioner

Head of the district administration. Overall in-charge of revenue, law & order, and development.

Additional Deputy Commissioner

Assists the DC in administrative duties, monitoring key revenue programs and schemes.

Assistant Commissioner

Oversees sub-divisional revenue matters, land acquisition, and coordination with taluk offices.

Tahsildar

Taluk-level administrative head responsible for land records, mutations, and local revenue.

Consultants

Technical experts providing support for e-governance initiatives and special projects.

Grade 2 Tahsildar

Supports the Tahsildar in daily operations, verification of documents, and field inspections.

Deputy Tahsildar

Assists the Tahsildar in field-level revenue administration and case management.

Revenue Inspector (RI)

Responsible for field-level revenue administration, survey work, and reporting.

Village Administrative Officer (VAO)

Village-level revenue official handling basic land records and local revenue collection.
